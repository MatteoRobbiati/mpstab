Citing mpstab
=============

If you use ``mpstab`` in your work, please cite the paper that presents it:

   Giulio Crognaletti, Mattia Robbiano, Michele Grossi, Matteo Robbiati,
   *MPStab: an hybrid stabilizers tensor-network quantum circuit simulator*.

BibTeX entry:

.. code-block:: bibtex

   @misc{crognaletti_mpstab,
     title  = {{MPStab}: an hybrid stabilizers tensor-network quantum circuit simulator},
     author = {Crognaletti, Giulio and Robbiano, Mattia and Grossi, Michele and Robbiati, Matteo},
   }

The hybrid stabilizer-MPO representation that ``mpstab`` implements was
introduced by Mello, Santini and Collura, `Phys. Rev. Lett. 133, 150604 (2024)
<https://journals.aps.org/prl/abstract/10.1103/PhysRevLett.133.150604>`_.
