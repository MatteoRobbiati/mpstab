Head Synthesis by Clifford Extraction¶

HQSMPO splits a circuit into a head, run on the device, and a tail, folded classically into the observable. The head is a list of dressed Pauli rotations, and has to become a circuit. This page explains the "extraction" synthesis, which needs no extra dependency and can be told the connectivity of the device.

The problem¶

A dressed rotation is exp(-i theta P / 2) with P a Pauli string, for instance Z Z X I. A device only rotates single qubits, so the string has to be brought onto one qubit first.

The textbook circuit does it in three steps:

  1. single-qubit gates turn every letter of P into Z;

  2. a chain of CNOTs collects the parity of the support on one qubit: each CNOT removes one site, so a string of weight w needs w - 1 of them;

  3. an RZ(theta) on that qubit, then steps 2 and 1 undone.

The undoing costs another w - 1 CNOTs, for every rotation.

The idea: do not undo¶

The gates of steps 1 and 2 are Clifford gates. A Clifford gate can be moved past a Pauli rotation: the rotation stays a Pauli rotation, only its string changes. So instead of undoing them, we leave them where they are and update the strings of the rotations still to come.

textbook      V1  RZ  V1^-1   V2  RZ  V2^-1   V3  RZ  V3^-1
extraction    V1  RZ          V2' RZ          V3' RZ           (V1 V2' V3')^-1

V  = the Clifford that brings a string onto one qubit
V' = the same, for the string as changed by the Cliffords before it

What is left at the end is one accumulated Clifford: the residual. It is never run. A Clifford turns a Pauli observable into another Pauli observable, so the residual is absorbed classically into what is measured, exactly as the Clifford part of the original circuit is.

This is the idea of QuCLEAR (Liu et al., arXiv:2408.13316): Clifford extraction (push them to the end) and absorption (handle them classically).

Which CNOTs¶

Bringing a string of weight w onto one qubit takes w - 1 CNOTs, but there are many ways to choose them, and each choice changes the coming strings differently. A good choice makes them lighter, a bad one heavier. This choice is the whole difference between a short head and a long one.

The rule used here is greedy. For the rotation in turn, among all the pairs (control, target) in its support, take the CNOT after which the next few strings touching those two qubits are

  • lightest: fewer sites, hence fewer CNOTs later;

  • most local: with a device connectivity, sites that are close on the chip, so that later CNOTs need no routing. A CNOT between distant qubits is itself charged the SWAPs that would route it.

Two more degrees of freedom are used:

  • rotations that commute may be reordered, the lightest first;

  • no single setting of the rule is best on every circuit, so a handful are tried (EXTRACTION_STRATEGIES) and the cheapest head is kept. A synthesis takes a fraction of a second.

Usage¶

from mpstab import HQSMPO
from mpstab.models.ansatze import ParametricKickedIsing
from mpstab.qpu import ExtractionSynthesis, PauliSampling

hs = HQSMPO(
    ansatz=ParametricKickedIsing(nqubits=6, n_steps=3, delta_h=0.2),
    # connectivity: None (all-to-all) | "line" | [(q0, q1), ...]
    head_synthesis=ExtractionSynthesis(connectivity="line"),
)
cut_index = len(hs.magic_gates) // 2

head = hs.resynthesize_head(cut_index)
print(head.method, head.n_two_qubit_gates)   # the circuit is head.circuit

# every hardware route uses that head and folds its residual
result = hs.expectation_at_cut("ZZIIII", cut_index, method=PauliSampling(), n_shots=20000, seed=0)
exact = hs.expectation_at_cut("ZZIIII", cut_index, method="simulation").value
assert abs(result.value - exact) <= 5 * result.total_error + 1e-6

The light cone of the observable¶

A second saving does not depend on the synthesis. Going backwards from the observable, a rotation matters only if its string anticommutes with the observable, or with a later rotation that matters. Every other rotation cancels against its own inverse in <psi| U^dag O U |psi>: it can be removed from the head, and need not be folded in the tail. This is exact, and costs only commutations of Pauli strings.

hs.light_cone = True                      # used by every hardware route
kept = hs.relevant_rotations("IIZIII")    # indices of the rotations that matter
head = hs.resynthesize_head(cut_index, observable="IIZIII")
assert len(kept) < len(hs.magic_gates)

It pays for local observables and shallow circuits. For a sum of terms the head keeps the union of their light cones, so an observable covering the whole chain (a sum of Z Z over every bond) removes nothing.

What to expect¶

Two-qubit gates after compilation to a line of CZ-native qubits, 16 qubits, head = 80% of the rotations (in brackets, the whole original circuit). The last column also drops the rotations outside the light cone of a Z on the central qubit:

                                    rustiq  original,  QuCLEAR  extraction  extraction
                                            truncated                       + light cone
kicked Ising, 8 steps       (240)     303      180       149        81          50
parametric kicked Ising     (240)     630      180       173       164          92
Trotter Ising, 8 steps      (240)     413      203       206       188         129
random brickwork, 6 layers  (390)     954      282       306       399         291

  • It pays where the circuit has a Clifford structure to extract (the kicked Ising chain near its Clifford point): the head is far shorter than the original circuit cut at the same place.

  • Where every gate is already a rotation (Trotter), there is nothing to extract, and the head is about as long as the original, slightly shorter.

  • On a random brickwork the rule is still worse than the original circuit: compare the candidates and keep the shortest.

  • The light cone helps most on shallow circuits: at 20 qubits and 4 steps of the kicked Ising chain, the same head goes from 42 to 10 two-qubit gates.

“Original, truncated” is the original circuit up to the cut, which is itself a valid head. It is the reference a synthesis has to beat. The light cone could be applied to it as well: the comparison of the last column with the others is not like for like.
